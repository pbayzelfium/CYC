Theme Catalogue

Every design rendered against the same short git repository, so what differs between these is the design and not the context. Pick a theme, pick a prompt, press Apply. The settings behind them live in the Terminal Control Room.

Opening this again. This page is a file on your machine, at ~\.oh-my-posh\catalogue\theme-catalogue.html. From any PowerShell window:

catalogue

It opened by itself when you installed, and refreshes itself whenever you add a theme with Install-TerminalTheme. Picking a theme or a design here and pressing Apply changes your terminal directly - nothing to copy or paste. Your browser will ask once whether to allow it.

Themes

A theme moves everything at once — window background, the 16 ANSI colours, the prompt, and the Claude Code status line. This is the full window, including the status line as it will look.
